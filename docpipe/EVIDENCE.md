## Gaps reported as findings

- **29148 clause-level text** was not retrievable open-access; E1 is grounded in INCOSE/PPI practitioner sources that cite it. If the ConOps/OpsCon split is challenged, buy the standard and cite Annex A.
- **No empirical evidence** was found for interview-driven (one-question-per-turn) document elicitation outperforming batch questionnaires with LLMs. The protocol is grounded in the house metaprompt's context-curation lever and Anthropic's guidance on explicit instructions; it is the first thing to A/B if T01/T02 evals score poorly.
- **Mermaid as OV-1 medium** is a convenience choice for a Markdown repo, not a standards position.
